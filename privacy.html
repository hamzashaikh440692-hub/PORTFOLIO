Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This is the personal website of Hamza Shaikh. This policy explains what information the site handles. In short: the site has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not set cookies of its own.

Information I collect

The site itself does not collect personal information. If you contact me by email, WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook, I receive whatever you choose to send, such as your name, contact details and project description. I use it only to reply to you and to discuss or carry out your project. I do not sell it or share it for marketing.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts: the site loads its fonts from Google. Your browser sends your IP address and browser details to Google to receive them. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Hosting provider: the company that hosts this site may keep server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, as part of running its service.
  • Links to GitHub, Instagram and Facebook lead to services with their own privacy policies. Messages you send there are also handled by those platforms.

How long I keep messages

I keep messages for as long as needed to answer you and to complete any project we agree on. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask what messages I hold from you, ask me to correct or delete them, or ask me to stop contacting you. Use any method on the contact page.

Changes

If the site starts using analytics, a contact form or anything else that handles personal data, I will update this page first. The date above shows the latest version.
